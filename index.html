Steady

An iPhone companion for emetophobia and gut-related anxiety.

Steady helps you practise living with uncertainty and discomfort, one small step at a time, at your own pace. It won't try to predict what your body will do.

What's in it

  • SOS tools: paced breathing, a cold reset, grounding and a guided audio exercise. They work with no signal.
  • A self-paced ladder: every step asks you first, and you can pause or step down at any time.
  • Habits: log safety behaviors, delay urges and try guided experiments.
  • Learn: five short lessons on how the gut and brain talk.

You stay in control

Trigger words are censored by default. There's no account and no tracking, and your logs stay on your iPhone.

Steady is in development and isn't on the App Store yet. It doesn't diagnose or treat medical conditions, and it doesn't replace a clinician.
