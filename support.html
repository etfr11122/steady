Steady Support

Tools for emetophobia and gut-related anxiety.

Get help with the app

Found a bug or have a question? Open a support request. Requests are public, so please don't include personal or health information.

Using Steady

  • SOS tools: they work with no signal, and you can open them from the lock screen.
  • Censoring: trigger words are censored by default. Change this in Progress › Profile.
  • Exposure steps: each one asks first, and Pause / Step Down is always on screen.
  • Moving to a new iPhone: your logs are left out of backups, so they don't transfer.
  • Deleting your data: go to Progress › Profile › Delete all my data.

Medical and safety

Steady doesn't diagnose or treat medical conditions, and it doesn't replace a clinician. Contact a doctor if you have severe pain in one area of your abdomen, a high fever, or any symptom that worries you once the panic has settled. Call your local emergency number if something feels urgent.

If you're thinking about harming yourself, or you're restricting food to the point of feeling unwell, please reach out to someone today. In the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, your local emergency number or a doctor can connect you with support.

Support requests aren't monitored for emergencies.
