Steady Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Steady offers tools for emetophobia and gut-related anxiety. It has no account and no sign-up, and the developer does not run any servers that receive your data.

The short version

  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your logs stay on your iPhone, and the app has no networking code.
  • Your logs are left out of iPhone backups.
  • We never sell or rent data.

Data stored on your device

Steady saves the following on your iPhone. Some of it is sensitive health information:

  • your ladder ratings and personal check-in answers,
  • exposure sessions, including their distress ratings, duration, volume and clarity,
  • SOS tool use and any distress ratings you give,
  • safety behaviors you log, delay timers and behavioral experiments, and
  • which lessons you've read.

The app does not send this information to the developer or to anyone else. It is encrypted by iOS Data Protection while your iPhone is locked, and it is left out of iCloud and computer backups, so it can't be restored to a new iPhone.

Your settings, such as the censor toggle, the lock setting and narration, are stored separately and may be included in backups. They contain none of your logs.

Deleting your data

Go to Progress, open Profile, and choose Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes your data from your iPhone.

Face ID

If you turn on the lock, iOS handles Face ID or your passcode. Steady never receives your face data or passcode.

Crash and usage statistics from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give the developer crash reports and statistics about app usage. These come from Apple, not from code in Steady. You can turn this off in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Steady is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
